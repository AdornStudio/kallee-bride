The salon

Gowns chosen without hurry.

Kallee Bride is the exclusive home of Lana Grace Bridal in Novi Sad. At our salon on Trg mladenaca every fitting is booked in advance and lasts as long as it needs to — no crowds, no pressure, and no sense that someone is waiting behind you.

We believe the right gown is never found in a rush. So we see one bride at a time, and give her the whole room, our full attention, and all the time she needs.

The fitting

What a fitting
with us looks like.

From your first message to the final alteration — you know exactly what happens at every step.

  1. 01

    Booking

    Write to us through the form or call. We confirm your appointment within one business day.

  2. 02

    Preparation

    Before you arrive we select gowns that suit your style, your figure and your wedding date.

  3. 03

    The fitting

    For that appointment the salon is yours alone. Try on as many as you like, with the people closest to you.

  4. 04

    Tailoring

    Once you have found your gown we take your measurements and guide you through every alteration.
